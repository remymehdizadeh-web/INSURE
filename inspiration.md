# What the preview borrows

Looked at the live homepages on 17 Oct 2026: Newfront, Vouch, Embroker, Lockton, Marsh, Stripe, Linear, Ramp, Mercury, and Vercel.

## Type and space

- Stripe, Ramp, and Mercury set one large line and then stop. The serif headline stays. Everything around it is smaller sans, with tight tracking on the display and almost none on the nav.
- Vouch and Linear keep the nav quiet: small type, one button, lots of unused width. The phone number stays, but it no longer competes with the button.
- Ramp and Stripe leave a wide gap between a headline, the sentence under it, and the action. The case numbers get that same gap.

## Photography

- Lockton and Marsh use wide architecture, not close crops. The new set is glass towers, a white tower, a dining room, a full construction site, a glass office, and a modern house, graded the same way: cooler, slightly desaturated, still sharp.
- Newfront puts the photograph behind a short line of type and lets the picture stay bright. The hero shade is lighter on the right so the towers read.

## Structure, not boxes

- Ramp and Vouch put a thin moving strip under the hero. Ours is coverage words, not carrier logos.
- Stripe and Linear use hairlines instead of cards. The last section is four lines with a rule between them.
- Motion stays small: the headline reveal, a slow photo drift, the horizontal practices, the coverage strip, and the existing premium count.
