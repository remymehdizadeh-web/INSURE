# Competitor findings (structure only)
Screenshots: ../website_preview_v4_research/, Oct 15 2026. Desktop 1440 and mobile 390. HUB desktop returned a Cloudflare block page; HUB mobile loaded. Structure notes only. No assets or text reused.

## What premium firms do
- Short headline, often 5 to 8 words. Marsh, Chubb, WTW and Alliant lead with a single statement and a serif typeface that reads like a firm, not a startup.
- One primary action above the fold: Connect, Explore, Get a quote. Not three competing CTAs.
- Photography is editorial and full bleed. Type sits on a dark scrim or a white panel next to it. No stock-happy-people feel on the best pages.
- Trust is concrete: license, markets, geography. No invented testimonials. Carriers rarely put logos of other firms on the home page.
- Cookie banners and chat widgets often wreck the fold. Keep chrome thin.

## What digital brokers do to convert
- Embroker, Next and Coverwallet put "Get a quote" within the first screen and map industries or products as chips.
- Forms are multi-step and short. Progress is visible. Phone is a secondary path, not a second sticky bar.
- Next uses a product grid of equal cards. Embroker pairs a photo with two clear buttons.

## Gaps we can beat for a NY commercial broker
- Most large brokers are vague ("solutions", "perspective"). We can be specific: buildings, restaurants, contractors, with a real Garden City office and a public DFS license.
- Almost none show a concrete anonymized savings outcome. A short, verified case study with real numbers is rare and persuasive.
- Sticky dual Call / Quote bars that also duplicate the hero CTAs feel cheap. One sticky bar on mobile only, none on desktop, and no hero Call button when the sticky bar already has Call.

## Apply to v4
1. Short serif headline, one-line subhead, quote start above the fold.
2. One sticky Call / Quote bar on mobile only. Hide header Call and hero Call on mobile.
3. Do not publish an unnamed restaurant result beside a named client. A dollar umbrella limit on the unnamed panel reads as the named client's limit.
4. Five line cards as a rail, three steps, one closing band. No invented stats.
